This is a really common experience, and there are actually a few reasons why you might feel like you look better in the mirror than you do in photos or on camera:

  1. Familiarity and Mirroring Effect: You’re used to seeing your reflection in the mirror, not your photographed image. The mirrored version is what your brain expects, so it feels "right" or more attractive. When you see yourself in a photo or on a camera, it’s a flipped image – little facial asymmetries can seem more noticeable because you’re simply not used to seeing yourself that way.
  2. Lighting Differences: Mirrors often reflect you in more flattering lighting—especially if you’re at home, where you might unconsciously seek out the best angles and light. Cameras, especially phone or computer cameras, often use less flattering lighting or exaggerate shadows and highlights, changing the way your features look.
  3. Camera Distortion: Most cameras use lenses that can slightly distort your face (making it look wider or longer, depending on the lens and distance). This effect is strongest with front-facing phone cameras, which can be especially unflattering compared to what you see in the mirror.
  4. Static vs. Dynamic: When you look in the mirror, you’re seeing yourself in motion, making subtle adjustments to your angle, expression, and posture. A photo or video freezes a single moment, sometimes catching you in an unflattering pose or expression.
  5. Psychological Expectations: You may be more forgiving of your appearance in the mirror because that's what you're used to. Seeing yourself in an unfamiliar format (like a photo) can be jarring because it doesn’t match your mental image.

You’re definitely not alone in feeling this way. Most people notice a difference between how they perceive themselves in mirrors and cameras. It’s not a sign that you actually “look bad” in photos—it’s just a mix of psychology and the technical quirks of cameras and reflections. Over time, you can get more used to your camera image, and those differences usually feel less striking!
