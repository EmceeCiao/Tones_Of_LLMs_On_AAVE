The first project that sent Americans into space was Project Mercury.

Project Mercury was the United States' first human spaceflight program, conducted by NASA. Its main goal was to put a human in orbit around the Earth and return them safely. The first American in space, astronaut Alan Shepard, flew on the Mercury-Redstone 3 mission (also known as Freedom 7) on May 5, 1961. Later, John Glenn became the first American to orbit the Earth on Mercury-Atlas 6 (Friendship 7) in February 1962.

So, Project Mercury is the correct answer.
