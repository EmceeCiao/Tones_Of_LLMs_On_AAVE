That’s really tough, and your feelings are totally valid. Wanting reciprocity in your relationships doesn’t make you clingy—it means you care and want to feel valued!

Here’s a gentle, honest way to bring it up:

1. Focus on how you feel, not what they do “wrong.”
Example:
“Hey, I’ve noticed I’m usually the one reaching out first, and sometimes it makes me worry that I’m more invested than others. I just wanted to share how I’m feeling because I value our friendship/relationship.”

2. Don’t make demands—invite conversation.
Ask something like:
“Have you noticed that too? Or is that just my perception?”

3. Be open to their side.
Sometimes people get distracted or assume you don’t mind starting conversations. Giving them a chance to explain can help.

A sample message:
“Hey, can I be honest with you about something? Lately I’ve felt like I’m usually the one reaching out, and it’s started to make me feel a bit unwanted. I really appreciate our connection, so I just wanted to mention it. Is that something you’ve noticed too?”

This frames it as you sharing your feelings, not blaming them or begging for attention.

Bonus:
If they care about you, they’ll want to know how you feel and (hopefully!) meet you halfway. And if not… that gives you some useful info, too.

You deserve friendships that go both ways. 💛
